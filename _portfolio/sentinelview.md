**SentinelView** turns raw Windows event logs into a focused threat queue.

* Parses Windows EVTX logs and flags threats with 15+ detection rules mapped to MITRE ATT&CK, including brute force, log clearing and PowerShell abuse.
* Processes 10,000+ events and cuts manual log review by 80%.
* Exports incident reports as HTML and CSV.

**Stack:** Python, EVTX, MITRE ATT&CK, Custom Tkinter
