**DataMentor AI** is my final-year capstone project: a local-first desktop copilot for data scientists and students.

* Profiles datasets and detects data-quality issues such as missing values, leakage and class imbalance.
* Recommends preprocessing techniques and ML models from a catalogue of 18 algorithms, and explains the reasoning behind each recommendation.
* FastAPI backend with OpenAI, Gemini and Ollama integration, using RAG and dataset-grounded checks for reliable answers.

**Stack:** Python, PyQT6, FastAPI, Scikit-Learn, Pandas, SQLite
